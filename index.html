Conference Paper: To what extent do reusable rockets reduce the financial, material and environmental costs of reaching space?

Abstract

Reusable rockets are often touted as a cheaper, more efficient way to reach space, but what about them actually makes them so? In this paper, we will assess the financial, environmental and material effects of using reusable rockets to reach space. Current evidence suggests that reuse can cut costs, especially at high launch rates, and may reduce material demand. However, factors such as payload loss and fuel choice limit these benefits and so the true benefits of reusuable rockets may remain uncertain. It is concluded that reusable rockets provide significant benefits when under the right circumstances.
