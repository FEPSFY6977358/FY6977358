Conference Paper: To what extent do reusable rockets reduce the financial, material and environmental costs of reaching space?

Abstract

Reusable rockets are often promoted as a cheaper, more efficient way of reaching space, though often the extent of these benefits remain unclear. This paper aims to assess the financial, material and environmental effects of launching reusable rockets. Currently, evidence suggests that reuse can cut costs, especially during high volumes of rocket launches and may reduce the material demand when it comes to building them. However, factors such as payload loss and fuel choice could limit these benefits. It is hence concluded that reuseable rockets offer significant benefits under the right situations.
